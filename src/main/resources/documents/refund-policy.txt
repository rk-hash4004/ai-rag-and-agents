REFUND POLICY

1. Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase (order ID or receipt).

2. Non-refundable items
- Gift cards
- Perishable goods
- Personalized or custom-made items
- Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout

3. Refund process
Once a return is received and inspected, we will notify the customer of the approval or rejection of the refund. Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to the original payment method.

4. Partial refunds
Partial refunds may be granted for items returned outside the 30-day window (but within 45 days) or items showing signs of use, at the discretion of the support team.

5. Late or missing refunds
If a refund hasn't appeared within 10 business days, customers should first check with their bank or card issuer, then contact support@example.com with their order ID.

6. Damaged or defective items
Items that arrive damaged or defective are eligible for a full refund or replacement at no additional cost, regardless of the standard return window, provided the issue is reported within 7 days of delivery.
