SHIPPING POLICY

1. Processing time
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days after payment confirmation. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are processed the next business day.

2. Shipping methods and delivery times
- Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days
- Express Shipping: 2-3 business days
- Next-Day Shipping: 1 business day (order must be placed before 2 PM local time)

3. Shipping costs
- Standard Shipping is free on orders over $50; otherwise a flat $5.99 fee applies.
- Express and Next-Day Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight.

4. International shipping
We currently ship to over 30 countries. International delivery times range from 7-21 business days depending on destination and customs processing. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.

5. Order tracking
A tracking number is emailed once the order ships. Tracking may take up to 24 hours to activate in the carrier's system.

6. Delayed or lost shipments
If a shipment doesn't arrive within the estimated delivery window plus 5 business days, contact support@example.com with the order ID to initiate a carrier investigation or reshipment.

7. Address changes
Shipping addresses can be changed only before an order enters "Processing" status. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be modified.
