CANCELLATION POLICY

1. Cancellation window
Orders can be cancelled free of charge within 1 hour of being placed, as long as the order has not yet entered "Processing" status.

2. After processing begins
Once an order enters "Processing" status, it can no longer be cancelled through self-service. Customers must contact support@example.com, and cancellation is granted only if the order has not yet shipped.

3. Orders that have shipped
Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled. Customers should instead follow the standard Refund Policy return process once the item is received.

4. Subscription cancellations
Recurring subscription orders can be cancelled at any time from the account dashboard. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle; no partial-period refunds are issued for subscriptions.

5. Cancellation by the company
We reserve the right to cancel an order due to product unavailability, pricing errors, or suspected fraudulent activity. In such cases, a full refund is issued automatically to the original payment method within 5 business days.

6. Refund timing for cancellations
Refunds for successfully cancelled orders are processed within 3-5 business days and follow the same refund method described in the Refund Policy.
